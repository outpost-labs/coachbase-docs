When you finish the assessment, you see your overall Practice Health Score, your band, and a snapshot of how each category scored.

![Results page showing the overall score and the email gate](/images/practice-health-score/03-results-gated.png)

## Your overall score

At the top, your **overall score** (0--100) appears with your band -- **Getting Started**, **Building Momentum**, **Well-Oiled**, or **Elite** -- and a short description of what that band means for your practice.

Below the score, a row of cards shows how each of the five categories scored, each with a quick label:

- **Needs Attention** (0--40)
- **Room to Grow** (41--60)
- **On Track** (61--80)
- **Thriving** (81--100)

## Unlock the full breakdown

To see the detailed breakdown and personalized recommendations, enter your first name and email address, then click **See My Full Results**. You can optionally share your coaching niche.

![The full breakdown with per-category recommendations](/images/practice-health-score/04-results-full.png)

The full results expand each category with:

- A description of what that score means for your practice
- A **How CoachBase helps** recommendation with a concrete next step
- A visual bar showing where you land in that category

{% callout type="note" %}
Entering your email unlocks the detailed recommendations. Your results stay private, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
{% /callout %}

## Share your results

Once unlocked, you can use **Share My Results** to share your results page. Each assessment has its own link, so you can revisit it later or compare after you have made changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What do I get for entering my email?
You unlock the full per-category breakdown, including what each score means and a recommended next step for every category.

### Can I retake the assessment later?
Yes. Take it again any time from **/tools/practice-score** to see how your score has changed as you improve your systems.

### Are my results public?
Only people with your unique results link can view your results. The page is not listed or searchable.
