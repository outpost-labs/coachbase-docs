The assessment walks you through 15 questions, one at a time, so you can focus on each answer without feeling overwhelmed.

![A Practice Health Score question with an answer selected](/images/practice-health-score/02-question.png)

## How it works

1. Go to **/tools/practice-score**.
2. Read the question and pick the option that best describes your practice today. Each question has five choices, ranging from "no system yet" to "fully systematized."
3. Click **Next** to move on. Use **Back** if you want to change a previous answer.
4. After the last question, click **See My Score** to view your results.

The progress bar at the top shows how far along you are (for example, **Question 1 of 15**) and the name of the category you are currently answering.

## Answer honestly

There are no right or wrong answers. The score is only useful if it reflects how your practice actually runs today, not how you wish it ran. Pick the option closest to your real workflow.

{% callout type="note" %}
Your progress is saved as you go, so if you get interrupted you can pick up where you left off.
{% /callout %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change an answer after moving on?
Yes. Click **Back** to return to any earlier question and update your choice before submitting.

### What if I am not sure which option fits?
Choose the option that is closest to your current reality. If you are between two, pick the lower one -- it keeps your score honest and highlights real opportunities.

### Do I have to answer every question?
Yes. All 15 questions are required so each of the five categories can be scored.
