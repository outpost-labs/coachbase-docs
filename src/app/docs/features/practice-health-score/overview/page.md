The **Practice Health Score** is a free assessment that scores how your coaching practice is running across five areas and shows you where you have the most room to grow. It takes about four minutes and requires no account.

![Practice Health Score start screen with the first question](/images/practice-health-score/01-start.png)

You can take it at **/tools/practice-score** -- no login needed. It is a great way to benchmark your practice before you commit to new tools or systems.

## What it measures

The assessment asks 15 questions, three in each of five categories:

- **Client Pipeline** -- how you find, track, and convert new clients
- **Session Management** -- how you schedule, deliver, and follow up on sessions
- **Financial Health** -- how you price, invoice, and collect payments
- **Client Experience** -- how clients work with you between sessions
- **Operations & Scale** -- the systems and tools that keep your practice running

Each category is scored from 0 to 100, and the five categories combine into a single overall score.

## Score bands

Your overall score places your practice in one of four bands:

- **Getting Started** (0--40) -- lots of growth potential; a few systems could transform how you work
- **Building Momentum** (41--60) -- you have a foundation, but gaps are costing you clients and revenue
- **Well-Oiled** (61--80) -- solid, with room to optimize; small improvements compound
- **Elite** (81--100) -- a best-in-class practice; time to scale what is working

{% callout type="note" %}
The assessment is anonymous to start. You only enter your email if you want to unlock the detailed category breakdown and recommendations.
{% /callout %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a CoachBase account to take it?
No. The Practice Health Score is a free, public tool. Anyone can take it without signing up.

### How long does it take?
About four minutes. There are 15 questions, shown one at a time.

### Is my score saved?
Yes. Each completed assessment gets its own results page with a unique link, so you can revisit or share it later.
