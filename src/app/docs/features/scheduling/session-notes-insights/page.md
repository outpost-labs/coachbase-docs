Keep a record of every session with built-in notes and let AI surface the key takeaways from your conversations.

## Session Notes

The session detail page includes a **Notes** section with a rich text editor.

![Session notes editor](/images/scheduling/15-session-notes.png)

### Editor Toolbar

The editor supports full formatting:

- **Bold**, **italic**, **strikethrough**, and **highlight**
- **Links** and **block quotes**
- **Headings** and **code blocks**
- **Ordered and unordered lists**
- **Images** and **tables**
- **Text alignment** options
- **Undo** and **redo**

### Text colors and highlights

Use the highlight tool in the toolbar to apply a **text color** or a **highlight** to selected text. Your color choices are saved with the rest of your formatting and stay in place when you reopen the session. To remove a color and return text to normal, select the text and use the highlight tool's reset option.

{% callout type="note" %}
Colors and highlights now persist when notes save, and can be cleared back to the default at any time.
{% /callout %}

### Auto-Save

Notes **auto-save** as you type. There is no manual save button — your notes are preserved automatically.

{% callout type="note" %}
Notes are private to you. Clients cannot see your session notes.
{% /callout %}

## AI Insights

Below the notes section, you'll find the **AI Insights** panel. It analyzes your session transcripts and surfaces a summary, categorized notes, and follow-up action items.

![Session detail page with notes and AI insights](/images/scheduling/11-session-detail.png)

Transcripts come either automatically from CoachBase video calls or from a file you upload or text you paste. AI Insights is available on the **Pro** and **Practice** plans.

{% callout type="note" %}
AI Insights has its own section of the docs. See **[Transcript AI Insights](/docs/features/transcript-ai-insights/overview)** for the full details — adding a transcript, reviewing what the AI generates, and replacing a transcript.
{% /callout %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are my session notes private?
Yes. Your notes auto-save and are visible only to you — clients cannot see them.

### Where do I learn more about AI Insights?
The [Transcript AI Insights](/docs/features/transcript-ai-insights/overview) section covers transcript sources, supported file formats, reviewing insights, and replacing a transcript.
