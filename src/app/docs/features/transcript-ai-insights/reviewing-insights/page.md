Once a transcript is analyzed, the **AI Insights** panel fills with everything the AI pulled from your session. Each part is yours to keep, act on, or remove.

![AI Insights showing a summary, categorized notes, and action items](/images/transcript-ai-insights/01-insights-ready.png)

## Summary

The **Summary** is a concise overview of the conversation — a quick way to recall what was discussed without re-reading the whole transcript.

## Notes

The **Notes** section lists key moments from the session. Each note carries a category badge — such as **Breakthrough**, **Observation**, or **Action Item** — and an **AI** label showing it was generated automatically.

To remove a note you don't want to keep, click the **×** next to it and confirm.

## Action Items

**Action Items** are concrete next steps the AI identified, saved as tasks for the client. Each shows the task title, an optional due date, and an **AI** label.

![Action items and suggested updates with Complete, Accept, and Dismiss controls](/images/transcript-ai-insights/05-suggested-updates.png)

For each action item you can:

- **Complete** — Mark the task done. Completed items show with a line through the title.
- **Remove** — Click the **×** to delete a task you don't need.

## Suggested Updates

When you analyze a new transcript for a session that already has action items, the AI may notice that the new conversation affects your existing tasks. Instead of changing anything on its own, it lists these as **Suggested updates** for you to review.

A suggestion might propose marking an existing task complete, or renaming it to reflect what changed. Each one includes a short rationale explaining why.

- Click **Accept** to apply the suggested change.
- Click **Dismiss** to ignore it and leave the task as-is.

{% callout type="note" %}
Nothing in Suggested updates changes until you approve it. Your existing tasks stay exactly as they are until you click **Accept**.
{% /callout %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens when I complete an AI action item?
The underlying client task is marked complete, just like any other task. The action item stays visible in the panel with its title struck through.

### Why am I seeing suggested updates?
Suggested updates appear when you analyze a new transcript and the AI believes it relates to action items you already have — for example, a task you discussed finishing. They're proposals only, so nothing changes without your approval.

### Can I edit the summary text directly?
The AI summary and notes are generated outputs. Use your session **Notes** editor above the panel to add your own observations or corrections.
