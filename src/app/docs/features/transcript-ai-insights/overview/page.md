The **AI Insights** panel reads your session transcripts and surfaces what matters — a summary of the conversation, categorized notes, and follow-up action items — so you can stay present in the session and let CoachBase handle the recap.

You'll find the AI Insights panel on each session's detail page, below the session notes.

![The AI Insights panel showing a summary, notes, and action items](/images/transcript-ai-insights/01-insights-ready.png)

## Where Transcripts Come From

There are two ways a session gets a transcript:

- **Video calls** — When you run a session through a CoachBase video call, the transcript is captured automatically. After the call ends, insights are generated without any extra steps.
- **Manual upload or paste** — For sessions held elsewhere (in person, over the phone, or on another platform), you can upload a transcript file or paste the text yourself.

A small badge on the AI Insights panel shows the source of the current transcript — either **Video Call** or **Uploaded**.

## What AI Insights Generates

Once a transcript is analyzed, the panel fills in with:

- **Summary** — A concise overview of what was discussed.
- **Notes** — Key moments from the conversation, each tagged with a category such as *Breakthrough*, *Observation*, or *Action Item*. AI-generated notes are marked with an **AI** label.
- **Action Items** — Concrete next steps pulled from the discussion, saved as tasks for the client with optional due dates.
- **Suggested updates** — When a new transcript affects action items you already have, the AI proposes changes for you to approve, rather than altering anything automatically.

{% callout type="note" %}
AI Insights is available on the **Pro** and **Practice** plans. If you're on the Solo plan, upgrade to unlock transcript analysis.
{% /callout %}

{% callout type="warning" %}
AI-generated insights are meant to support your judgment, not replace it. Always review the summary, notes, and action items for accuracy before acting on them or sharing them.
{% /callout %}

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a video call to use AI Insights?
No. Video calls generate transcripts automatically, but you can also upload a transcript file or paste transcript text for any session held elsewhere.

### Which plans include AI Insights?
AI Insights is included on the Pro and Practice plans. It is not available on the Solo plan.

### Can clients see my AI Insights?
No. The summary, notes, and action items in the AI Insights panel are part of your private session record.
