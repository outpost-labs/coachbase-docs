---
title: "Browser Notifications"
description: "Get a desktop or phone alert for new client messages, bookings, and cancellations when CoachBase isn't open in front of you."
---

Browser notifications are an optional extra channel on top of the in-app notification center and email. When a client messages you, books a session, or cancels one, your browser shows a notification even if CoachBase is in a background tab or closed.

They never replace email. Every notification that matters still reaches your inbox as before.

## Turning them on

The first time you use CoachBase in a browser that supports notifications, a small prompt appears beneath the notification bell a few seconds after the page loads. Click **Turn on** to enable them, or **Not now** to dismiss the prompt. Dismissing it is remembered, so you won't be asked again in that browser.

You can also enable or disable them at any time from the **Notifications** page (click the bell, then **View all**). The **Browser notifications** section shows whether they're on for the current browser.

Clicking **Turn on** or **Enable browser notifications** opens your browser's own permission dialog. Notifications only work once you've allowed them there.

## What you're notified about

- A new message from a client
- A new booking through your public booking page
- A client cancelling a session

Notifications about a message show the client's name as the sender and the first line of what they wrote, so you can tell an urgent message from a thank-you without opening CoachBase. Several messages from the same client replace each other rather than stacking up.

Clicking a message notification opens that conversation in the [Inbox](/docs/features/inbox/overview). Other notifications open the relevant page.

## When they fire

Browser notifications are only sent when no CoachBase window is visible. If you're actively working in the app, the in-app toast and badge are enough, so nothing pops up on top of them. Look away, lock your laptop, or switch to another app, and the next message pushes to your device straight away.

## Phones and tablets

On Android, notifications work in Chrome and most other browsers once you've allowed them.

On iPhone and iPad, Safari only supports browser notifications for sites added to the Home Screen. Open CoachBase in Safari, tap the Share button, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then open CoachBase from that icon and turn notifications on. CoachBase doesn't show the prompt in browsers that can't deliver notifications, so if you don't see it on your phone, that's why.

On macOS, Safari uses its own generic icon for browser notifications unless CoachBase has been added to the Dock as a web app.

## Turning them off

Use the **Notifications** page to disable them for the current browser, or revoke the permission in your browser's site settings. Either way, email and in-app notifications continue as normal.

## If notifications aren't arriving

- Check that the browser has permission. In Chrome, click the lock icon in the address bar; in Safari, open Settings for the site.
- Notifications are per browser and per device. Turning them on in Chrome on your laptop doesn't turn them on in Safari or on your phone.
- Operating system focus modes such as Do Not Disturb hide notifications from every site, including CoachBase.
