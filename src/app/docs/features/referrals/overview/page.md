The **Referrals** page lets you earn subscription credits by inviting other coaches to CoachBase. When a referred coach subscribes to a paid plan, you receive a $20 credit toward your own subscription.

![Referrals page showing the payback calculator, referral link, and stats](/images/referrals/01-overview.png)

## The payback calculator

At the top of the page, the calculator shows what referring is worth to you. Pick a number of coaches you could invite and watch the credit total update — alongside a comparison against your own plan price, so you can see exactly how many referrals cover your subscription for the month. Two referrals cover a Solo plan; three cover Pro; five cover Practice.

## Your dashboard card

Your dashboard also shows a referral card with how close you are to covering your plan this month (for example, "2 more referrals covers your Solo plan"). You can copy your referral link straight from the card, or dismiss it if you'd rather not see it.

## Your referral link

At the top of the page, you will find your unique referral link. Click **Copy link** to copy it to your clipboard and share it with other coaches via email, social media, or messaging.

When someone signs up using your link:

- They get their **first month free**
- You earn a **$20 credit** once they subscribe to a paid plan

## Tracking referrals

Three stat cards show your referral performance at a glance:

- **Total referrals** -- the number of coaches who signed up using your link
- **Qualifying referrals** -- referrals where the coach subscribed to a paid plan
- **Total credits earned** -- the dollar value of credits applied to your account

## Referral history

If you have referrals, a history table appears below the stats showing each referral with:

- **Referral** -- the referred coach's name or email
- **Referred on** -- the date they signed up
- **Status** -- **Signed up** (registered but no subscription), **Trialing** (on a trial or has payment info), or **Subscribed** (active paid plan)
- **Credit** -- $20.00 for qualifying referrals, or a dash if not yet qualified

## How credits work

{% callout type="note" %}
Referral credits apply to your subscription invoices only. They cannot be used to cover payment processing fees, and they can't be cashed out.
{% /callout %}

Credits are applied to your subscription invoices automatically and roll forward until used — if you earn more than one month's cost, the remainder carries to future invoices. You need an active paid subscription for credits to be redeemed, and there is no limit on the number of referrals you can earn credits for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When do I receive my credit?**
Your $20 credit is applied once the referred coach subscribes to a paid plan (not during their free trial).

**Is there a limit to how many coaches I can refer?**
No. You can refer as many coaches as you like and earn $20 for each one who subscribes.

**Can I see who clicked my link but did not sign up?**
No. Only coaches who complete registration appear in your referral history.
