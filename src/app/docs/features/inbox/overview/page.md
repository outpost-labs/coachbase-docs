---
title: "Inbox"
description: "One place for every client conversation, with unread tracking, reactions, and resend for messages that couldn't be emailed."
---

The Inbox collects every conversation with your clients in one place. It lives in the top navigation next to the notification bell, and it is where new client messages arrive in real time.

The Inbox is available on accounts with the Client Portal enabled. Clients write to you from the [portal](/docs/features/client-portal/files-and-messages) or by replying to your emails, and both land in the same conversation.

## Layout

The Inbox is a two-pane workspace. Your conversations sit on the left, sorted with the most recent activity first. The open conversation fills the right pane. Each pane scrolls on its own, so a long thread never pushes the list off screen.

On a phone or a narrow window the two panes collapse into one. Tap a conversation to open it, and use the back arrow at the top to return to the list.

A dot beside a client's name marks a conversation with something you haven't read. The **Show** menu in the top right switches between all conversations and only the unread ones.

## Reading and replying

Messages are grouped under date dividers. When you open a conversation it scrolls to the first message you haven't read, marked with a **New messages** line, so you pick up where you left off.

Send times are one gesture away rather than printed on every bubble. Drag the conversation sideways, or swipe on a touch screen, and each message reveals when it was sent.

Type your reply in the box at the bottom and press **Send**, or use Cmd/Ctrl+Enter. The box grows as you type. Your reply is delivered to the client's portal and emailed to them, and their email reply routes straight back into the same conversation.

## Marking a conversation unread

Sometimes you read a message but can't act on it yet. Hover over any client message and choose **Mark unread from here**. The conversation goes back to unread from that message onward, so the dot returns and the thread reopens at that point next time.

## Reactions

Hover over a message to add an emoji reaction. Reactions appear for both you and the client the moment they're added, on whichever screen each of you has open.

## When an email couldn't send

Every reply you send is also emailed to the client. If the email is rejected, the message shows an **Email couldn't send** label with a **Send again** action. Sending again queues the email again without creating a duplicate message in the conversation.

## Notifications

New client messages don't go to the notification bell. Instead the Inbox icon shows a badge with the number of unread conversations, and a small toast appears in the corner if you're working elsewhere in CoachBase. If the conversation is already open in front of you, the message simply appears in the thread and is marked read.

If you're away from CoachBase, [browser notifications](/docs/features/notifications/browser-notifications) can reach you on your desktop or phone. Whether or not you have those turned on, an unread client message is emailed to you after a short delay if you haven't read it: 3 minutes when browser notifications are off, 15 minutes when they're on.

## What stays out of the Inbox

Session confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notices are sent to the client by email only. They no longer appear as system messages inside the conversation, so the thread only contains what the two of you actually said to each other.

## Opening the client record

The three-dot menu at the top of a conversation offers **View contact**, which opens the client's record on the Messages tab, and **Copy client email**.
